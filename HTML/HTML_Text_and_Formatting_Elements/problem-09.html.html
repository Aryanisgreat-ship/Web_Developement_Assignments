Student Terms & Rules

General Rules

1. Students must carry their valid student ID card while attending classes and other institutional activities.

2. Students must maintain cleanliness in classrooms, laboratories, and other common areas.

Attendance

3. Students must maintain the minimum required attendance to be eligible for examinations.

4. Students must attend classes regularly.
Absence should be supported by a valid reason when required.

Note: Attendance requirements may vary according to institutional policies.

Examination

5. Students must follow all examination instructions given by the examination authorities.

6. Students must not use unauthorized materials during examinations. Violation may result in disciplinary action.

Assignment Submission

7. All assignments must be submitted before the given deadline. Late submissions may receive a penalty.

Note: Students should keep a backup of their submitted assignments.

Discipline

8. Students must behave respectfully towards teachers, staff members, and fellow students.

9. Bullying, harassment, and disruptive behaviour are strictly prohibited. Serious violations may lead to disciplinary action.

Important Notice

All students are expected to read and follow these rules.

These rules are intended to maintain a safe, respectful, and productive learning environment for everyone.
