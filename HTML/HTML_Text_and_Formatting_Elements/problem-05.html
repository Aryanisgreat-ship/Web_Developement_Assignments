Science Notes

These notes contain important concepts and scientific formulas from Chemistry, Physics, and Biology.

Chemistry

Water: The chemical formula of water is H2O.

Carbon Dioxide: The chemical formula of carbon dioxide is CO2.

Oxygen: Oxygen normally exists as a molecule represented by O2.

Sulfuric Acid: The chemical formula of sulfuric acid is H2SO4.

Ammonia: The chemical formula of ammonia is NH3.

Atoms are the basic units of matter, while molecules are groups of atoms bonded together.

Physics

Area: The SI unit of area is square metre, represented as m2.

Volume: The SI unit of volume is cubic metre, represented as m3.

Scientific Notation: Large and small numbers can be represented using powers of 10, such as 103 and 10-6.

Force is a physical quantity that can change the motion or shape of an object.

Energy: Energy is the capacity to do work. Its SI unit is the joule (J).

Biology

Cell: The cell is the basic structural and functional unit of life.

DNA: DNA stores genetic information required for the growth and development of living organisms.

Photosynthesis: Plants use carbon dioxide (CO2) and water (H2O) to produce food in the presence of light.

Respiration: During cellular respiration, organisms use oxygen (O2) to release energy from food.

Chlorophyll is the green pigment in plants that plays an important role in photosynthesis.
