5 Injured as 5.9 Magnitude Earthquake Rattles Tokyo, Eadtern Japan; Trains Distrupted

Strong Tremors Hit Tokyo as Injuries and Train Disruptions Are Reported

A 5.9-magnitude earthquake struck eastern Japan, including the Tokyo region, early Sunday, August 23, 2026. The Japan Meteorological Agency said the epicentre was in southern Ibaraki Prefecture at a depth of around 70 kilometres.Strong tremors were felt across Tokyo and nearby areas, triggering emergency alerts on mobile phones.


The earthquake initially caused reports of at least five injuries, although later updates from regional authorities raised the reported total to 37 people across five prefectures. Most injuries were described as minor. The quake also caused limited damage, including a ruptured underground water pipe in Tokyo's Koto Ward and temporary power outages affecting around 460 homes.


Transportation was also affected, with delays reported on several express and local train services, including routes connecting Tokyo with Narita Airport. However,Japan's Shinkansen bullet trains continued operating normally. Authorities confirmed that there was no tsunami threat, while Japan's nuclear regulator reported no abnormalities at nuclear facilities. Officials have urged residents to remain cautious because further aftershocks are possible.
