GLA UNIVERSITY

About The College

GLA University is a private state university located in Mathura, Uttar Pradesh, established in 2010. It offers diploma, undergraduate, postgraduate, and doctoral programs across engineering, management, pharmacy, law, and sciences, holding an A+ grade from NAAC and 12B status from the UGC.

Courses Offered

GLA University offers a wide range of undergraduate, postgraduate, and doctoral programs across engineering, management, computer applications, pharmacy, science, and humanities. Key programs include B.Tech, BBA, MBA, BCA, MCA, B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture, and various M.Tech and Ph.D. options.

Campus Life

Campus life at GLA University blends a disciplined, tech-enabled academic atmosphere with active student engagement. The environment features modern air-conditioned amphitheater-style classrooms, high-speed Wi-Fi connectivity, extensive central libraries, and diverse cultural, technical, and sports activities across its campuses.

Why I Like My College

I love GLA University because it perfectly balances academic focus with an exciting, high-energy student culture.
The modern, tech-enabled campus feels like a home away from home, especially with the vibrant cafeteria hubs and comfortable hostel life. Between the massive annual festivals, diverse club activities, and constant sports events, there is never a boring day on campus.
Most importantly, the supportive professors and tight-knit community create a great environment where I can build lasting friendships and confidently prepare for my future career.
