Before Practical Class

  1. Read the practical instructions.
  2. Bring the required notebook and materials.
  3. Reach the laboratory on time.
  4. Follow the instructor's instructions.
  5. Submit the practical work before leaving.

Classroom Rules

  • Students must attend classes regularly.
  • Maintain discipline inside the classroom.
  • Keep mobile phones on silent mode.
  • Do not damage laboratory equipment.
  • Submit assignments on time.

Important Notice: Internal Assessment submissions must be completed before the deadline.

Academic Terms

Lecture
A theoretical teaching session.
Lab
A practical session conducted in a laboratory.
Tutorial
A session focused on practice and problem solving.
Internal Assessment
Assessment conducted during the academic semester.
Practical
A hands-on academic activity.

Faculty Message

Students are encouraged to attend classes regularly and actively participate in academic and practical activities.
