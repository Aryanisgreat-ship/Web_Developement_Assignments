ADMISSION OVERVIEW

Welcome to the GLA University admission handbook. This page provides important information about eligibility, application process, documents, fees and admission terms. "Choose the right program, complete your application carefully and follow all admission instructions."

ELIGIBILITY

Students must fulfill the eligibility requirements of their selected program before applying for admission. Mandatory Requirement: Candidates must submit correct academic and personal information during registration.
