  1. Visit the official registration page.
  2. Select your competition.

  4. Submit the registration form.
  5. Save your registration reference number.

Fest Rules

  • Students must carry their college ID card.
  • Participants must report before the scheduled time.
  • Maintain discipline at all venues.
  • Follow instructions given by coordinators.
  • Damage to college property is prohibited.

Important Terms

Open Event
An event open to eligible participants.
Team Event
An event in which participants compete as a team.
Individual Event
An event where one participant competes individually.
Registration
The process of officially entering a competition.
Coordinator
The person responsible for managing an event.
Final Round
The last competitive stage of an event.
