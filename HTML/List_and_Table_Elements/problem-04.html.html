Laptop Comparison Guide

Choosing the right laptop depends on your processor, RAM, storage, display, battery, warranty and intended use.

How to Choose Your Laptop

  1. Decide your main purpose.
  2. Select a suitable processor.
  3. Choose enough RAM for your work.
  4. Select SSD storage according to your needs.
  5. Compare display and battery specifications.
  6. Check price and warranty.

Technical Terms

RAM
Temporary memory used by the computer for running applications.
SSD
Fast storage device used for storing operating system and files.
CPU
The main processor responsible for executing instructions.
GPU
Processor responsible for graphics-related tasks.
Refresh Rate
The number of times a display refreshes every second.
Battery Capacity
The amount of energy the laptop battery can store.
