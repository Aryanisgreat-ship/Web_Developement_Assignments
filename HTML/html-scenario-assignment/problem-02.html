University Opens New Innovation and Research Center

New Facility Aims to Encourage Student Research and Innovation

A new Innovation and Research Center was officially opened at the university campus on 24 August 2026. The center was inaugurated during a special ceremony attended by university officials, faculty members, students, and invited guests. The facility is designed to provide students with better opportunities for practical learning and research.

The new center is located at the university's main campus in New Delhi. It contains modern laboratories, research rooms, collaborative workspaces, and seminar facilities. Students from different departments will be able to use the center for academic projects, experiments, and research activities.

The university has invested ₹25 crore in the development of the new research center. According to university officials, the facility is expected to support thousands of students every year. The center will also provide opportunities for students and teachers to work together on research and innovative projects.

The center was established to promote innovation, practical education, and collaboration between different academic departments. University officials believe that giving students access to modern facilities will help them develop useful technical and problem-solving skills. This initiative is expected to strengthen the university's focus on practical and research-based education.
