Hiring Process

Our hiring process is designed to understand your skills, experience, problem-solving ability and cultural fit.

  1. Application Submission
  2. Resume Screening
  3. Technical Assessment
  4. Technical Interview
  5. HR Interview
  6. Final Selection
  7. Offer and Joining

Hiring Stages Explained

Screening
Recruiters review your resume, experience, education and skills against the requirements of the position.
Technical Round
Candidates may be asked programming, technical, analytical or role-specific questions.
HR Round
The HR discussion focuses on communication, career goals, availability and overall suitability for the organization.
Offer
Selected candidates receive an employment offer containing details about the position and joining process.
