How to Request an Appointment

  1. Choose the required department or specialist.
  2. Check the available consultation schedule.
  3. Complete the appointment request form.
  4. Provide accurate contact information.
  5. Select your preferred date and time.
  6. Submit the appointment request.
  7. Wait for confirmation from the clinic.
  8. Arrive at the clinic at the confirmed time.

Common Healthcare Terms

Consultation
A professional interaction between a patient and healthcare professional for assessment, guidance and appropriate care.
Follow-up
A subsequent consultation intended to review progress or continue an existing care plan.
OPD
OPD stands for Outpatient Department, where patients receive consultation or services without hospital admission.
Appointment
A scheduled time reserved for a patient consultation or healthcare service.
