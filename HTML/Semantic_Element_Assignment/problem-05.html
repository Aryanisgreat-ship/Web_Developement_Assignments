Event Overview

The FuturePath Career & Education Fair 2026 is a large-scale career and education event designed to help students and job seekers discover employment, internship, scholarship and higher-education opportunities.

The event brings together companies, universities, training organizations, government institutions and career experts to provide participants with direct access to information and opportunities.

Participants can meet recruiters, explore career options, attend expert sessions, submit resumes and learn about different educational and professional pathways.

Event Details

Date: 18 October 2026

Time: 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM

Venue: Grand Convention Centre, New Delhi

Entry: Free for registered participants

Objectives of the Fair

  • Connect students with employers and educational institutions.
  • Provide information about internships and placements.
  • Introduce participants to scholarship opportunities.
  • Help job seekers understand current industry requirements.
  • Provide career preparation and interview guidance.
  • Promote skill development and lifelong learning.

Organizer's Message

"Our goal is to give every participant meaningful access to career information, industry professionals and educational opportunities so that they can make confident decisions about their future."

FuturePath organizers encourage participants to arrive early and actively participate in workshops and networking sessions.

Preparation Guide

Proper preparation can help you make the most of your career fair experience.

Preparation Tips

  • Prepare an updated resume.
  • Research participating organizations before the event.
  • Prepare a short professional introduction.
  • Carry multiple copies of your resume.
  • Dress appropriately for professional interactions.
  • Prepare questions for recruiters.
  • Keep your important academic information available.
  • Arrive at least 30 minutes before your selected sessions.

Attendee Journey

  1. Register for the career and education fair.
  2. Receive your registration confirmation.
  3. Arrive at the venue and complete check-in.
  4. Attend the opening and orientation session.
  5. Visit participating organization booths.
  6. Attend workshops and career sessions.
  7. Speak with recruiters and education representatives.
  8. Submit applications or collect relevant information.
  9. Follow up with organizations after the event.

Important Participant Notices

Notice: Carry a valid identification document for event registration and verification.

Recruiter Tip: Introduce yourself confidently and keep your first conversation concise and professional.

Resume Tip: Highlight skills and projects that are relevant to the organization you are visiting.

Important: Some organizations may conduct on-site screening. Check individual organization requirements before attending.

Information & FAQs

Important Career Terminology

Career Fair
An event where employers, educational institutions and career organizations meet students and job seekers.
Internship
A temporary learning and work opportunity that provides practical experience in a professional environment.
Placement
The process through which an eligible candidate receives an employment opportunity from an organization.
Scholarship
Financial assistance provided to eligible students to support their education.
Apprenticeship
A structured training program that combines practical workplace experience with skill development.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who can attend the event?

Students, graduates, experienced professionals and job seekers can register for the fair.

Is registration required?

Yes. Participants should complete the online registration form before attending the event.

Should I bring my resume?

Yes. Participants are strongly encouraged to bring updated copies of their resume.

Can students look for internships?

Yes. Several participating organizations may provide internship and training opportunities.

Can I attend multiple sessions?

Yes. Participants may attend multiple sessions as long as the session timings do not overlap.
