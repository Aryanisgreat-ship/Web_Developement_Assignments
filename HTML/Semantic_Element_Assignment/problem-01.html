Academic Programs

Students can choose from a variety of undergraduate and postgraduate programs designed to develop academic knowledge and professional skills.

Bachelor of Technology (B.Tech.)

The B.Tech. program develops engineering knowledge, programming skills, problem-solving abilities and practical technical expertise.

Eligibility: Candidates must satisfy the required 10+2 academic qualification and applicable subject requirements.

Learn More About B.Tech.

Bachelor of Computer Applications (BCA)

The BCA program focuses on computer applications, programming, databases, web development and software technologies.

Eligibility: Candidates must have completed the required 10+2 qualification or equivalent.

Learn More About BCA

Master of Business Administration (MBA)

The MBA program develops management, leadership, communication, analytical and entrepreneurial skills.

Eligibility: Candidates must possess a recognized bachelor's degree and satisfy applicable admission requirements.

Learn More About MBA

Application Process

Follow these steps to complete your university application:

  1. Explore available programs.
  2. Check program eligibility.
  3. Choose your preferred program.
  4. Register for the admission process.
  5. Complete the application form.
  6. Appear for the required entrance test, if applicable.
  7. Check the admission result.
  8. Complete fee payment and document verification.

For the official step-by-step procedure, visit: GLA University Admission Procedure

Important Admission Terms
