About Tech & Career Expo 2026

Event Introduction

The Tech & Career Expo 2026 is a large technology and career event designed to connect students, graduates, educators, recruiters and technology professionals.

Participants can explore opportunities in Artificial Intelligence, Software Development, Cybersecurity, Cloud Computing, Data Science, Electronics, FinTech and other emerging technologies.

The event provides opportunities to attend career workshops, technical sessions and recruitment activities.

Message from the Organizer

Welcome to Tech & Career Expo 2026. Our goal is to create a platform where students can discover career possibilities, interact with organizations and learn directly from industry professionals.

"The future belongs to those who continuously learn, adapt and create."

We encourage every participant to ask questions, build professional connections and make the most of every session.

Important Event Notice

Participants should carry their registration confirmation and a valid student or professional identification document.

Notice: Workshop seats may be limited and will be provided on a first-come, first-served basis.

Important Career Terms

Career Expo
An event where students and professionals meet organizations to explore career and employment opportunities.
Internship
A temporary learning and work experience that provides practical exposure to a professional environment.
Placement
The process of connecting eligible candidates with employment opportunities.
Workshop
An interactive educational session focused on developing specific knowledge or practical skills.
Certification
A credential that demonstrates completion of a course, training program or assessment.
Networking
The process of building professional relationships with students, recruiters, speakers and industry professionals.

Attendee Journey

  1. Register for the Tech & Career Expo.
  2. Check your registration confirmation.
  3. Review the event schedule and select sessions.
  4. Research participating organizations.
  5. Prepare your resume and professional profile.
  6. Attend workshops and speaker sessions.
  7. Visit company booths and ask questions.
  8. Network with recruiters and professionals.
  9. Apply for suitable internships or career opportunities.
  10. Follow up with organizations after the expo.

Preparation Tips

Prepare Before the Expo

  • Update your resume.
  • Prepare a short professional introduction.
  • Research participating companies.
  • Carry copies of your resume if required.
  • Prepare questions for recruiters.
  • Review your technical skills.
  • Choose workshops according to your career goals.
  • Dress appropriately for professional interactions.
  • Keep your registration confirmation available.
  • Arrive early for important sessions.

Career Tip

Do not only ask recruiters about available jobs. Ask about required skills, career paths, internships and learning opportunities.
