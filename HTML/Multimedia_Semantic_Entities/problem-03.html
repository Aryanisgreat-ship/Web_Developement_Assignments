Destination Overview

Welcome to Agra, one of India's most famous historical destinations. Agra is home to magnificent monuments, rich culture, traditional cuisine and unforgettable travel experiences.

The city is best known for the magnificent Taj Mahal, a UNESCO World Heritage Site and one of the world's most recognizable monuments.

Travel Tips

Follow these useful tips to make your Agra trip comfortable and enjoyable.

  • Start sightseeing early in the morning.
  • Carry drinking water during summer.
  • Wear comfortable walking shoes.
  • Keep your valuables safe.
  • Respect local customs and heritage sites.
  • Book accommodation in advance during peak season.
  • Keep digital and physical copies of important documents.

One-Day Agra Itinerary

Follow this simple one-day plan to experience some of Agra's most popular destinations.

  1. 6:00 AM - Visit the Taj Mahal at sunrise.
  2. 9:00 AM - Have breakfast at a local restaurant.
  3. 10:00 AM - Explore Agra Fort.
  4. 1:00 PM - Enjoy traditional Mughlai lunch.
  5. 2:30 PM - Visit Itmad-ud-Daulah's Tomb.
  6. 4:00 PM - Explore local markets.
  7. 6:00 PM - Watch the sunset from Mehtab Bagh.

Visitor Information

Here are some common travel and visitor services that tourists may need during their trip.

Check-in
Most hotels provide check-in during the afternoon. Carry a valid identity document when checking in.
Guided Tour
Visitors can hire professional guides to learn about the history and architecture of Agra.
Local Transport
Auto-rickshaws, taxis, buses and app-based transportation services are available.
Travel Pass
Visitors should check available tourism passes and monument tickets before travelling.
