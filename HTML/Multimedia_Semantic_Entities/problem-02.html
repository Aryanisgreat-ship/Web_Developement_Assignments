Recruitment Process

Our recruitment process is designed to identify talented candidates and understand their technical and professional abilities.

Hiring Stages

  1. Submit your application
  2. Resume screening
  3. Technical assessment
  4. Technical interview
  5. HR interview
  6. Final selection
  7. Offer and onboarding

Recruitment Stage Details

Screening
Our recruitment team reviews your resume, qualifications and relevant experience.
Technical Round
Candidates participate in technical assessments and interviews related to their selected role.
HR Round
The HR team discusses communication skills, career goals, expectations and company culture.
Offer
Selected candidates receive an employment offer containing role, salary and joining information.
