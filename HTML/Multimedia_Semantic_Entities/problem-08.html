Bank Accounts

Savings Account

A savings account allows customers to deposit money and may provide interest on the balance. It is commonly used for emergency funds and short-term savings.

  • Keep an emergency fund.
  • Monitor your account regularly.
  • Understand minimum-balance requirements.
  • Compare interest rates and service charges.
Learn about banking awareness

Current Account

Current accounts are generally designed for frequent transactions and are commonly used by businesses and organizations.

Check transaction limits, maintenance charges and other applicable fees before opening an account.

Cards and Responsible Credit

Debit Card

A debit card generally allows you to spend money directly from your linked bank account.

  • Protect your PIN.
  • Never share OTPs.
  • Check transaction notifications.
  • Report unauthorized transactions quickly.

Credit Card

A credit card provides access to a predefined credit limit. The amount used must be repaid according to the card agreement.

Paying the full outstanding amount on time can help avoid unnecessary interest charges.

Never borrow more than you can comfortably repay.

Understanding Loans

Personal Loan

A personal loan is borrowed money that is repaid through scheduled payments. The total cost depends on factors such as principal, interest rate, fees and tenure.

Home Loan

A home loan is generally used to finance the purchase or construction of a property. Such loans may have a longer repayment period.

Before taking a loan, compare the interest rate, processing fees, repayment period and total repayment amount.

Responsible Borrowing

  • Borrow only what you need.
  • Calculate your monthly repayment ability.
  • Compare different lenders and products.
  • Read all loan terms and conditions.
  • Keep sufficient money available for repayments.

Important Financial Terms

Interest Rate
The percentage charged on borrowed money or earned on certain deposits over a specified period.
EMI
Equated Monthly Instalment. It is a regular payment made toward repaying a loan.
Principal
The original amount of money borrowed or deposited, excluding interest.
Tenure
The period over which a loan is repaid or an investment/deposit remains active.

A simplified EMI calculation may involve the formula: EMI = P × r × (1 + r)n / ((1 + r)n − 1)

Responsible Personal-Finance Tips

Daily Financial Habits

  • Create a monthly budget.
  • Track your income and expenses.
  • Save a portion of your income regularly.
  • Avoid unnecessary debt.
  • Pay bills and EMIs on time.
  • Maintain an emergency fund.
  • Compare fees before selecting financial products.
  • Protect your financial and personal information.

Financial Awareness

Even a small monthly saving can become significant over time. For example, saving ₹5,000 every month can contribute to a stronger emergency fund.

Remember: higher returns may involve higher risks. Understand the product before committing your money.

Steps for Opening an Account

  1. Choose the type of account you need.
  2. Compare available financial products.
  3. Read eligibility requirements and fees.
  4. Complete the application form.
  5. Provide the required information and documents.
  6. Complete the applicable verification process.
  7. Review the account terms and conditions.
  8. Activate and securely manage your account.
