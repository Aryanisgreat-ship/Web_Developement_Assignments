Our Restaurant Story

Spice Garden was founded in 2015 with the goal of bringing traditional flavors and modern cooking together.

We use fresh ingredients and carefully selected spices to prepare every dish. Our food represents a perfect combination of tradition and innovation.

We believe that good food brings people together & creates unforgettable memories.

"Great food is not just about taste; it is about the experience shared with people."

Learn more about food and cooking at BBC Good Food .

Meet Our Chef

Chef Arjun Mehta

Chef Arjun has more than 15 years of culinary experience. He specializes in Indian, Asian and contemporary cuisine.

His cooking philosophy is based on fresh ingredients, traditional recipes and creativity.

Signature dish: Royal Spice Biryani
