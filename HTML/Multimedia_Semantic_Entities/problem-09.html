Welcome to GLA Sports Academy

About the Academy

GLA Sports Academy is a training and development center dedicated to helping athletes improve their physical fitness, technical skills, discipline and sportsmanship.

Our programs are designed for beginners as well as experienced athletes. Training focuses on fitness, discipline, teamwork and competitive performance.

"Success is the result of preparation, hard work and learning from failure."

Our academy welcomes athletes who want to learn, compete and develop their sporting abilities in a positive environment.

Academy Notice

Athletes must follow academy safety rules, coach instructions and tournament regulations at all times.

Important: Training schedules may change because of weather, tournaments or facility availability.

Training Programs

Beginner Training Program

Designed for athletes who are starting their sports journey. The program develops basic movement, coordination, fitness and fundamental sporting skills.

  • Warm-up and stretching
  • Basic fitness exercises
  • Fundamental sports techniques
  • Team activities
  • Basic match practice

Advanced Training Program

This program is designed for experienced athletes preparing for competitive tournaments.

  • Advanced technical training
  • Strength and conditioning
  • Speed and agility
  • Tactical preparation
  • Competitive match practice

Fitness & Conditioning

Athletes work on stamina, flexibility, strength, balance and recovery techniques.

Example training calculation: 5 sessions × 60 minutes = 300 minutes of weekly training.

Training Steps

  1. Complete athlete registration.
  2. Select your preferred sport and category.
  3. Attend the initial fitness assessment.
  4. Meet the assigned coach.
  5. Follow the training schedule.
  6. Participate in practice sessions.
  7. Take part in internal assessments.
  8. Participate in tournaments when selected.

Important Sports Terms

Match
A competitive game or contest between two athletes or teams.
Fixture
A scheduled match between specified teams or competitors.
League
A competition in which teams or athletes compete across multiple scheduled matches.
Knockout
A tournament format where a competitor is eliminated after losing a match.
